## 10. Open design questions (the inventory's list; the ticket settles them or asks)

1. The scope's unit name (R-050, R-060, R-035 must recognise it): `limen-<job id>.scope`; `limen-job-<token>.scope`; a
   generated `run-….scope` recorded in the record.
2. What runs in the scope: the wrapper (and so the engine, `--prepare`, `ps`, `afterEnd`'s children); only the engine;
   the wrapper with the engine in a sub-scope.
3. Who stops the scope at a terminal transition: the winner of `finalizeJob` as its last step; only actors outside the
   scope; `systemctl --user kill` replacing the group signals (overlaps R-027, R-028). `afterEnd`'s detached children
   would die with the scope.
4. A host without a user instance: refuse (exit 4, `REQUIREMENTS`); run unscoped with an advisory; refuse except under
   a test knob.
5. The peaks: `memory.peak` and `cpu.stat` `usage_usec` read once before the scope stops; sampled during the run;
   both; one optional object or separate fields.
6. `oom`: `oom_kill` at the end against the start; watched; the unit's result; R-026's or R-028's.
7. The coordinator: never in a job scope (R-050); team coordinators are jobs; a job that spawns jobs gets a new scope
   per wrapper or stays in its parent's.
8. Placement: the default user slice; a `limen.slice`. `systemd-run --user` needs the user-bus variables, which R-087's
   allow-list must keep for jobs that spawn jobs.
9. The fake: PATH shims plus a fake cgroup root; a platform-adapter member with injected dependencies; both.
