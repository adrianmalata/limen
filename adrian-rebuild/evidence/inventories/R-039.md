### 3.3 Confirmed in the vendor copy of Pi 1.5.1 (this inventory's reads)

- `sendCustomMessage` (pi:`dist/core/agent-session.js:1946-1984`): `nextTurn` → `_pendingNextTurnMessages`;
  streaming and `triggerTurn !== false` → `agent.followUp` or `agent.steer`; idle and `triggerTurn` → `_startRun`
  (held while `agent_settled` is being emitted); streaming and `triggerTurn: false` → `_pendingCustomMessages` (flushed

  prompt changed. A message returned by `before_agent_start` or sent as `nextTurn` lands **after** the prompt.
