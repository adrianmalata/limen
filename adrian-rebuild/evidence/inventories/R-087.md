  entry.kind === "removed")` plus `values`, whose keys must be transport names (`:229-235`). It drops no name outside
  the registry, and keeps every `test` and `exception` name. Its comment (`:238-239`): "so a variable that a
  coordinator's shell or a job's engine holds never reaches the job" (true of transport names only).
